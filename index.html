Research

Pricing when shoppers ration their attention

Shoppers compare prices only where comparing pays. My model prices a whole menu by that attention, with one formula.

First-author paper under review at Operations Research; a related paper on learning from conversion data is at minor revision.

Spending simulation budget where the decision changes

When the best decision depends on context, most contexts are easy. CONE spends simulation runs where the decision is close.

INFORMS Journal on Computing, 2026, with Y. Shen and L. H. Lee · doi:10.1287/ijoc.2025.1134

Staffing queues that do not reset

Waiting callers carry a surge into the next half hour. Relocate–Certify–Repair staffs for that, with a certified reliability guarantee.

Working paper with S. Shu and C.-P. Teo.
