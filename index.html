Research & Deployed Systems

A

Airline Revenue Prediction Platform

Lead researcher

End-to-end simulation platform forecasting revenue over 1-week to 1-year selling horizons under competitor pricing, RM policy, and network-level capacity competition. Trained and deployed a Transformer-based deep choice model on customer browsing and transaction data; built the full-stack booking simulator (Django + React, MongoDB, Celery) end-to-end with AI-assisted development. Adopted by the airline’s practitioners to validate pricing and RM decisions before deployment.

  • Deep choice model
  • Simulation
  • Django + React
  • Deployed with partner
B

Checked-Baggage Ancillary Pricing

Project lead

Ran randomized price experiments across 1.41M booking sessions and five baggage tiers, established through reduced-form analysis that substitution is sharply heterogeneous, and built a rational-inattention demand model that prices the whole tier ladder from a handful of interpretable parameters — calibrated by linear programming so it scales to thousands of sparse segments. Randomized field deployment against the incumbent rule raised baggage revenue ~8% (p < 0.01), with the recommended price distribution first-order stochastically dominating the incumbent: posted prices fall on average even as revenue rises. Now running in production at the partner airline; first-authored write-up under review at Operations Research.

  • Revenue management
  • Rational inattention
  • Randomized field experiment
  • In production
C

NUHS JMC Call-centre Rostering

Project lead

Discrete-event simulation and optimization model for agent rostering that cut the call-abandonment rate from 6% to 4%; now in live use for monthly staff planning. The method — Relocate–Certify–Repair for non-resetting queues, with an exact finite-sample reliability certificate — meets the abandonment target in 565 of 570 weekday-period cells on fresh replications.

  • Discrete-event simulation
  • Optimization
  • Deployed
D

National Heart Centre Singapore — Appointment Scheduling

Project lead

Appointment-scheduling optimization under demand uncertainty to uphold the centre's lead-day promise — booking patients within the committed lead time while absorbing demand variability.
