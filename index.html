Research

Pricing a menu when shoppers ration attention

One price moves shoppers across a whole menu. My model sets every price by how closely shoppers watch it.

First-author paper under review at Operations Research; a related paper on learning from conversion data is at minor revision.

Learning the best decision for every context

Operations need a rule: the best option for each context. CONE learns it by simulation, spending runs on close calls.

INFORMS Journal on Computing, 2026, with Y. Shen and L. H. Lee · doi:10.1287/ijoc.2025.1134

Staffing queues that do not reset

Rostering half-hour slots while calls and patience shift and callers carry over. Certified service, with staff placed ahead of congestion.

Working paper with S. Shu and C.-P. Teo.
